Description

The Embraer A-29 Super Tucano is a turboprop light attack and advanced training aircraft developed from the EMB-312 Tucano. Designed for counterinsurgency, close air support, border surveillance, and pilot training, the aircraft combines rugged construction with modern avionics and low operating costs. It is capable of operating from short and unprepared runways and is optimized for missions in austere environments.

The A-29 features reinforced landing gear, armor protection for crew and critical systems, night-vision compatible cockpit, and advanced sensors. Its combat-proven performance has led to adoption by numerous air forces worldwide, making it one of the most successful light attack aircraft of its class.
